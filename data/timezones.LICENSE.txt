Vendored data attribution - UTC timezone offset bands
======================================================

File:        timezones.geojson  (FeatureCollection "timezones_wVVG8")
Source:      treyerl/timezones - https://github.com/treyerl/timezones
License:     MIT
Retrieved:   2026-06-18
Projection:  WGS84 / EPSG:4326 (lng, lat)
Content:     UTC offset bands. Each feature's only property `name` is the offset
             string in hours (e.g. "-6", "+1", "-9.5"); geometry is Polygon /
             MultiPolygon. 38 offset-band features.
Filtered:    The upstream file ships 39 features; one ("what3") is a null-geometry
             sentinel that carries no offset band and renders nothing. It was
             removed so every feature is a real offset band (the label-is-offset
             contract). No band geometry was altered. Offsets present: -12 .. +14.

Usage in this project
---------------------
Served at the runtime path /data/timezones.geojson and lazy-fetched (never
statically bundled) by the investigator globe's opt-in "Time zones" overlay, which
draws a thin offset-boundary line, a translucent fill and a "UTC+1" label per
band, and highlights the band holding the selected record
(src/lib/globe/tzBands.ts). The day/night terminator
drawn alongside is computed locally from the playhead (src/lib/terminator.ts) and
is NOT derived from this dataset.

This NOTICE is kept next to the asset (mirroring the OFL-*.txt font licenses in
public/fonts/) per the project data-integrity rule: vendored-data provenance and
license are recorded, never papered over. A clickable credit also appears under
the "Time zones" switch in the settings gear menu (src/components/ThemeSwitcher.tsx).

Known approximations (measured 2026-10-05 against tzBandAt)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Standard offsets only: no DST, and no +12:45 band, so Chatham (-176.5, -43.9)
  resolves to +12.
- Band seams do not always match and some bands overlap in slivers. Of all
  38034 ring vertices, 37794 fall in exactly 1 band, 104 in 2 or more and 136
  in none (e.g. (172.5, 50.8846) between +11 and +12). A point on such a seam gets no
  highlight, or the first matching band in file order (Timor edges, the Fergana
  enclaves). Interior city points resolve correctly: Dili +9, Fergana +5,
  Osh +6, Yangon +6.5, Kathmandu +5:45.
