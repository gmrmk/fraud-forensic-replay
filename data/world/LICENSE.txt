Vendored data attribution: world ocean, land borders and country label points
=============================================================================

File:        world.json
Source:      Natural Earth, 1:110m cultural and physical vectors, as GeoJSON in
             https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector at tag v5.1.2:
             ne_110m_ocean, ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land, ne_110m_admin_0_countries
License:     public domain. No permission is needed to use Natural Earth
             (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Made with Natural Earth.
Retrieved:   2026-11-14, by scripts/fetch-basemap-assets.mjs (run by hand; the app never runs it)
Projection:  WGS84 / EPSG:4326 (lng, lat)
Content:     one object with three FeatureCollections.
             ocean:   2 polygons, geometry only.
             borders: 331 land boundary lines, geometry only.
             labels:  177 points, one per country, with one property, `name`, taken from the
                      source's NAME field at its LABEL_X and LABEL_Y position.
Reduced:     every other property is dropped and positions are rounded to two decimals of a
             degree (about 1.1 km, finer than the source's scale). No shape was added.

Usage in this project
---------------------
Served at the runtime path data/world/world.json. The investigator globe draws it under the
map tiles while they load, and keeps it when the tile host cannot be reached, so the globe
shows ocean, borders and country names without waiting for tiles. It is hidden once the
tiles are in. Boundaries and names follow Natural
Earth's de facto policy and are a loading placeholder, not a statement by this project.
