Vendored basemap files: style, sprites and glyph ranges
=======================================================

Retrieved:   2026-11-14, by scripts/fetch-basemap-assets.mjs (run by hand; the app never runs it)
Source:      OpenFreeMap, https://openfreemap.org (styles kept at
             https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles)
Licences:    licenses/ in this folder holds three upstream licence files, verbatim.
             openfreemap-styles-LICENSE.md names the style's licences and links to the
             BSD 3-Clause and CC BY 4.0 texts. osm-liberty-LICENSE.md is the licence file
             of OSM Liberty, the style's upstream, fetched by hand on 2026-11-14 from
             https://github.com/maputnik/osm-liberty (branch gh-pages, LICENSE.md): it holds
             the BSD conditions and disclaimer the style keeps from OSM Bright (the
             copyright line they refer to is in OSM Bright's own licence file, not stored
             here) and OSM Liberty's design, icon and font credits (its font line names
             Roboto; the style stored here uses Noto Sans). noto-fonts-OFL.txt is the text
             of the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Style
-----
File:        src/lib/globe/basemap/liberty.json (bundled into the app, not served from here)
What:        the "Liberty" style as served at https://tiles.openfreemap.org/styles/liberty,
             unchanged. The app replaces its sprite and glyphs addresses with this site's
             own at run time and restyles some layers per theme.
Origin:      a fork of OSM Liberty (https://github.com/maputnik/osm-liberty), itself a fork
             of OSM Bright.
License:     code BSD 3-Clause, design CC BY 4.0 (see licenses/openfreemap-styles-LICENSE.md;
             the BSD conditions and disclaimer are in licenses/osm-liberty-LICENSE.md)

Sprites
-------
Files:       sprites/ofm.json, sprites/ofm.png, sprites/ofm@2x.json, sprites/ofm@2x.png
What:        the style's sprite sheet, unchanged.
License:     Maki POI icon set, CC0 1.0 Universal; the right arrow is public domain
             (see licenses/openfreemap-styles-LICENSE.md)

Glyphs
------
Files:       fonts/Noto Sans Regular/, fonts/Noto Sans Bold/, fonts/Noto Sans Italic/,
             each with the ranges 0-255, 256-511, 512-767 and 8192-8447 (.pbf)
What:        signed-distance-field glyph ranges of Noto Sans as OpenFreeMap serves them,
             unchanged. Basic Latin and Latin-1, Latin Extended-A and B, IPA and spacing
             marks, general punctuation. A label that needs another range has it requested
             from the tile host.
License:     SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see licenses/noto-fonts-OFL.txt)

Not stored here
---------------
Map tiles. They are requested from tiles.openfreemap.org at run time. The credit shown in
the app is: OpenFreeMap, (c) OpenMapTiles, data from OpenStreetMap.
